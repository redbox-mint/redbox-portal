# Fictional Research Data Stewardship Guide

This document is synthetic demonstration material. It is not institutional, legal, or ethics advice.

## Storage

Use an institution-managed research storage service with access limited to the project team. Keep direct identifiers separate from research content and maintain encrypted backups.

## Sharing

Prefer open aggregate outputs. Before releasing de-identified participant-level data, review consent, ethics conditions, and disclosure risk. Record any access conditions in the data management plan.

## Retention

Confirm the applicable retention period with the researcher. At the end of the period, review continuing value and securely dispose of data that no longer needs to be retained.

## Ethics

Do not infer ethics approval. When the supplied project context does not establish the approval and consent position, flag the statement for researcher review.
