# Waterline quirk register

Single list of Waterline-era behaviours found during migration, under
TX-PARITY-01/01a ([design](design.md)). Each entry is either **preserved**
(the replacement reproduces it; candidate for a later fix) or **improved** (the replacement's
natural behaviour is strictly better; tested and recorded), or an **accepted**
Prisma quirk explicitly decided in ADR 0004. Strengthening a
preserved quirk later needs its own decision.
