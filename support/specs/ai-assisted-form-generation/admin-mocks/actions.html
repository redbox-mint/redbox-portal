Design notes. GenerationBinding is called a form action here: admins think in terms of “which button shows where”, not bindings. The empty state explains the concept in one sentence and is the most likely first visit. The list reads left to right as a sentence: button on record creates record for roles using profile. The enable switch is inline because turning a button off is the common incident response. Delete is only offered for actions that have never run; otherwise ⋯ offers Disable.
