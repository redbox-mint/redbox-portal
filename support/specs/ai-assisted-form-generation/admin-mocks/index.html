Design notes. One sidebar entry (“AI-assisted drafting” under Integrations) opens a single embedded app with tabs, as the design spec proposes. The overview is a checklist because the five entities depend on each other in a fixed order, and admins otherwise can’t tell why the button isn’t showing. Steps link to the exact place to continue; a step whose prerequisite is missing is disabled with a reason. Environment-backed generation.config.ts values are shown read-only with their variable names so brand admins understand limits they can’t change. The sidebar tag reads “Setup” until a binding is live, then “On”.
