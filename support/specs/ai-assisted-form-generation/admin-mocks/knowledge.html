Design notes. “Knowledge collection” is labelled Guidance: admins upload policy, not a knowledge base. Documents carry the authority, effective dates and tags the model needs, shown as badges so admins can spot a mis-tagged document. Expiring documents are flagged before they silently drop out. “Try it” is the test-retrieval endpoint with a field picker, so admins check retrieval in the same terms profiles use. Drafts follow the same versioning language as models and profiles.
