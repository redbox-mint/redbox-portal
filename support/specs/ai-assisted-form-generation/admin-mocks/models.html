Design notes. The data model splits GenerationModelConnection (where/how) from GenerationModelDeployment (which model, which settings, immutable versions). The UI keeps that split but names it plainly: a connection is reusable credentials; a model is what profiles choose. Connections are cards because there are few and their health matters at a glance; models are a table because they version. Data-handling is shown in words with who verified it and when (the dataPolicy JSON), because it is the first thing a privacy reviewer asks. Providers that the operator installed but no one configured get a prompt card. Published versions are not editable: the row action is “New version”, which copies to a draft.
