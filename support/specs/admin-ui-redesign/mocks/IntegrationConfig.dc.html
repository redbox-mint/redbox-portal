This one layout serves Figshare (today about 8,900px), DOI (about 8,100px) and Oni publishing. The 25 raw schema groups fold into about 12 named sections in a sticky rail. Collapsed sections show a one-line summary of their current values, and the rail flags sections with problems. Secrets render as password fields with a stored-secret hint (DOI’s password is plaintext today). Arrays such as status codes and methods become chip inputs rather than rows of number fields with red × buttons.
