## Deliberately excluded packages and later qualification

- JSON parsing uses Fastify's built-in parser. Nested form/query behavior needs
  explicit `qs` configuration: formbody's default flat parser is insufficient.
  Bounds, charset/error behavior, parser scope and denial order remain FM-010/016/020.
- Multipart retains the owned `StreamingMultipart`/`@fastify/busboy` pipeline.
  Native scoped content-parser APIs can preserve raw request streams for multipart,
  TUS and Companion. No new multipart/raw-body plugin is needed to buffer them;
  the required ownership/cancellation/staging tests remain FM-020/033/034/036.
- Socket.IO attaches to `fastify.server`, and Companion has its dedicated upgrade
  boundary under design §11. No `@fastify/websocket` or Socket.IO wrapper plugin:
  no separate WebSocket application protocol is called for.
- CSP nonces/directives, canonical-origin handling, opener isolation and asset
  headers already have concrete helpers in `src/pages/csp.ts` and
  `src/http/express/pageHeaders.ts`/`pageAssets.ts`. Design §8/§9 requires their
  behavior to survive. No Helmet defaults or extra global security policy is
  introduced; the transport port/qualification remains FM-029/031.
- Native Passport versus direct provider bindings is a P2 selection. No speculative
  Passport, CORS, CSRF, secure-cookie-session or Mongo session-store plugin is added.
  Cookie/session selection does not qualify Mongo ownership, TTL/index handling,
  rotation/revocation, raw-response saves, auth providers or rollback semantics.
- No `@fastify/express`, `@fastify/middie` or other general Express bridge is added.
  Existing Express composition and Companion dependencies remain for their current
  entrypoint and planned later cutover work.
