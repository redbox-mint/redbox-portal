## Fixed workload and measurement contract

The [FM-005 method](FM-005.md#fixed-representative-workloads-for-fm-007-and-fm-047)
is implemented without changing its payloads, timings or concurrency:

- W1: bearer + API v2 metadata GETs round-robin across all 1,000 measured records;
  concurrency 1 and 8; full v2 metadata/oid/revision validation.
- W2: strict PUT `?merge=true`, exactly 1,024-byte alternating title/padding patch;
  concurrency 1 and 4 on disjoint records. Seed ETags are fetched before timing,
  each PUT consumes its new ETag and must advance revision by one. Zero unexpected
  refresh GETs. Persisted metadata hashes, revisions, audit rows and updated Solr
  titles are verified after drain.
- W3: authenticated HTML record view, concurrency 1 and 8, round-robin measured
  records. Login/CSRF/session setup is outside timing. Expected title, record
  identity and form bootstrap must be present; login redirects cannot pass.
- W4: authenticated 1 MiB and 32 MiB FS byte streams, concurrency 1 and 4 each.
  Every transfer is fully consumed and hash-verified. Full completion and TTFB
  are separate measurements. Deterministic SHA-256 values are
  `dd89e74c8ccbce6061f9419d556bd2dade72e7cd6838dab9ad712c949148d96e`
  and `11520f9d99d3ef079aeed6224c411ed18dd936fb2da0c782b600573c5a9ae03b`.

Each sample drops four disposable databases, resets queues/audits/records/Solr
indexes and attachment disks, and starts a fresh owner. It seeds exactly 1,000
measured + 100 separate warm records, with 8,192-byte UTF-8 metadata at revision
zero, using the real storage service and selected form/workflow/ACL. Initial
Solr bulk seeding uses the real preIndex/SolrDocument mapping outside timing.
Real mutations retain queued search work (four workers/locks, Agenda 0.1-second
polling). Warmup is 30 seconds; its audits/queues/index effects are cleared and
all 1,000 measured seed revisions/hashes reverified before the 120-second timed
window. There is one persistent HTTP/1.1 connection per closed-loop worker,
30-second request deadlines, a shared 60-second late-completion/owned-drain budget
and a 600-second outer attempt timeout. Streams are unthrottled.

Latency is nearest-rank p95 over **all successful offered work**, including late
completions. Timed throughput uses only full completions by 120 seconds; late
counts/bytes/latencies remain separately visible. Unexpected errors, wrong
fields/bytes/revisions, fewer than 100 timed completions, undrained/failed queues
or leaked pools invalidate a sample. The complete selected batch passed all of
these checks. No duration extension or pooling was needed.
